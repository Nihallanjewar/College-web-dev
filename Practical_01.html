About Department:

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering offers industry - oriented education in courses like Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Data Science, Software Engineering and Cybersecurity.
The Department also provides access to modern laboratories, library and latest technology.

Achievements

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering has consistently promoted academic excellence, innovation,
and technical development. Over the years, the department has achieved several milestones
in education, research, and student success.

  1. NBA Accredited Program
  2. 80+ Research Publications
  3. Excellent Academic Results
  4. Teams selected for National and International Hackathons

Placements

With a focus on innovation and continuous learning, the department has contributed significantly to
