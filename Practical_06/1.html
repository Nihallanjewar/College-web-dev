About the Event

TechNova is the yearly technical festival of our university. It is a 2 day event where students from different colleges come together to show their skills in coding, robotics, gaming and many more things. There are also fun events, prizes and certificates for everyone. Come and join us!

Last year more than 1000 students took part and everyone enjoyed a lot.

Quick Info

Date: 20-21 Nov 2026
Time: 9 AM to 5 PM
Entry: Free for our college
Others: Rs. 100

Activities

💻 Code Sprint

Solve coding problems in 2 hours. Languages allowed: C, C++, Java, Python.

🤖 Robo Race

Build a robot that can finish the track first. Teams of 3 members.

🎮 Gaming Zone

Play and win in BGMI, Valorant and FIFA tournaments.

🌐 Web Design Contest

Make a nice website using HTML, CSS and Bootstrap in 90 minutes.

🧠 Tech Quiz

Questions on computers, gadgets and general tech knowledge.

🎤 Cultural Night

Singing, dance and a DJ night on the evening of Day 2.
