About the Event

TechFest is the yearly technical festival of our university. It is a 2 day event where students from
different colleges come together to show their skills in coding, robotics, gaming and many more
things. There are also fun events, prizes and certificates for everyone. Come and join us!

Last year more than 1000 students took part and everyone enjoyed a lot.
